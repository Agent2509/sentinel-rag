## 1. Demo Flow (10 Minutes Total)

### Act 1: The Fast Path (Trace View — 2 mins)
* **Goal:** Prove fast routing and hybrid retrieval + RRF.
* **Page:** `http://localhost:3000/` (Trace View)
* **Settings:** Strategy = `semantic`, Self-Correction = `ON`.
* **Query:**
  ```text
  What is LoRA?
  ```
* **What Happens:**
  - Route: Classified as `simple` (or direct lookup).
  - Hybrid Retrieval: Top chunks retrieved via FAISS + BM25 with RRF scores displayed.
  - Grader: Instant high score (`> 0.85`).
  - Answer: Grounded answer with citations `[1]`, `[2]`.
* **Talk Track:**
  > "Notice the trace: the system first routed the query, retrieved candidate chunks combining dense vector similarity with sparse BM25 keyword matching, graded them for sufficiency, and generated an answer with strict inline citations."

---

### Act 2: The Self-Correction Loop (The "Hero" Feature — 3 mins)
* **Goal:** Show the self-correcting HyDE retry loop (Amber pulse -> Recovery).
* **Page:** `http://localhost:3000/` (Trace View)
* **Settings:** Strategy = `semantic`, Self-Correction = `ON`.
* **Query:**
  ```text
  How does attention compare to recurrence for long range dependencies?
  ```
* **What Happens:**
  - Step 1: Routing -> `complex`.
  - Step 2: Initial retrieval.
  - Step 3: Grader evaluates passages.
  - Step 4 (The Reveal): If relevance score is low or ambiguous, the pipeline triggers **HyDE Rewrite**.
  - Amber retry card appears: `hyde_rewrite` synthesizes a hypothetical document.
  - Step 5: Second retrieval using hypothetical document embedding.
  - Step 6: Grader re-evaluates -> Green success glow -> Final grounded answer.
* **Talk Track:**
  > "This is where normal RAG hallucinates. Sentinel's Grader flagged the initial chunks as insufficient. Instead of failing silently, it triggered an agentic HyDE loop, rewrote the retrieval vector, re-retrieved, and recovered autonomously."

---

### Act 3: Graceful Fallback / Unanswerable (2 mins)
* **Goal:** Prove no hallucination on out-of-domain input.
* **Page:** `http://localhost:3000/` (Trace View)
* **Settings:** Strategy = `fixed`, Self-Correction = `ON`.
* **Query:**
  ```text
  What is the corporate revenue and stock price of Microsoft in 2024?
  ```
* **What Happens:**
  - Grader fails. HyDE retry fails.
  - Final status: `not_found`.
  - Clean fallback message: *"I couldn't find a confident answer in the corpus. Here are the closest chunks I found:"*
  - Shows closest chunks for transparency. Zero hallucination.
* **Talk Track:**
  > "When the answer isn't in the corpus, Sentinel doesn't invent facts. It gracefully degrades, admits uncertainty, and shows you the receipts."

---

### Act 4: Strategy Comparison Lab (1.5 mins)
* **Goal:** Compare chunking strategies side by side on identical queries.
* **Page:** Click **Strategy Lab** in navbar (`http://localhost:3000/strategy`).
* **Settings:** Left = `fixed`, Right = `hierarchical`.
* **Query:**
  ```text
  Why do transformers use layer normalization instead of batch normalization?
  ```
* **Talk Track:**
  > "Chunking strategy dictates retrieval performance. Fixed chunks often cut across sentence boundaries. Hierarchical chunking searches small 256-token children for precision but supplies the 1024-token parent context to the generator."
* **After the run:** point at the **delta strip** under the panels — latency winner, retries, and status side by side. That comparison is the point of the page.

---

### Optional Act: Live Document Upload (30s)

* **Page:** Click **Documents** in navbar (`http://localhost:3000/documents`).
* **Action:** Drag `sample_docs/quantum_key_distribution.md` onto the drop zone → the corpus badge grows and the reindex spinner appears. Delete it again to show the confirmation dialog and the clean rollback.
